FAQ

Questions before you pilot.

Is our data used to train any models?

No. Your documents and query data stay inside your deployment and are never used to train Kratu's or any third-party models.

Can Kratu be self-hosted?

Yes. You can run Kratu entirely inside your own infrastructure, or let our team host and manage it for you. Both options ship with the same security and audit guarantees.

How does Kratu handle access control?

Every user and AI agent action passes through role-based access control at the API layer. Denied and allowed requests are both logged for review.

What happens after the free beta?

Pilot teams get full platform access at no cost for the beta period. Afterwards, you move to a flat monthly plan with no per-seat pricing — we'll walk you through it before anything changes.

How long does deployment take?

Most pilots are live within a week: connecting your data sources, configuring roles, and training your team on day-to-day use.
